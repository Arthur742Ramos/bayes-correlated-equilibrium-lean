# Independent mathematical review

A separate Codex reviewing agent performed a read-only mathematical comparison
against Bergemann–Morris Theorem 1 and the intermediate proof snapshot. It found
no substantive correctness issue. The final source review below covers subsequent
module-export and supporting-lemma changes.

The model permits finite heterogeneous spaces, arbitrary real utilities, and
nonnegative normalized kernels. Expansion is a real joint kernel with the exact
original-signal marginal. The BNE gain fixes the player's action and multiplies
only opponents' independent behavioral probabilities. The recommendation
construction is substantive; reverse factorization and summing extra own signals
is noncircular and covers genuinely mixed strategies. Zero masses are handled
without conditional beliefs. The quotient/joint-law equivalence has the paper's
exact positive-original-kernel support condition.

Documented qualifications: BCE denotes obedience, while the main theorem supplies
probability validity. The paper's full-support prior is relaxed. The existential
uses the action universe, with the reverse theorem polymorphic in extra-signal
universe. Theorem 2 requires separate all-games outcome-order and individual
sufficiency arguments and a substantial converse, and remains unproved.

No human review or original-author endorsement is claimed. The reviewer ran no
builds and did not replace the transitive axiom audit.
